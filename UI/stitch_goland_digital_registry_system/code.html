The Problem

Why GoLand Exists

India's current land registration system is a fragmented labyrinth. Records are scattered across three separate departments—Revenue, Survey, and Registration—making cross-verification nearly impossible for the average citizen.

This administrative friction results in an average transaction time of 90 to 120 days. Most alarmingly, the lack of a single source of truth enables massive malpractice, with over ₹6,000 crore lost to property fraud annually.
